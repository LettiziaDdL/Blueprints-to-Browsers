Frequently asked questions

Do I need a consultation first?

Most treatments require a consultation first so we can talk through your goals and plan your treatment together.

Why is there a deposit?

A deposit reserves your appointment time. The deposit amount is shown on each service page.

Are the prices final?

Treatment prices are starting prices. Your final price depends on the plan we make together during your consultation.

What about aftercare?

Your provider will give you personalized aftercare instructions at your appointment. If you have questions afterward, contact us using the details above.

How is my health information handled?

Health history and treatment details are handled privately during your consultation. They are not collected in the online checkout form.
